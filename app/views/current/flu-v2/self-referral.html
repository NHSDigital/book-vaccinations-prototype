able to get a flu vaccine?

As 're under 65, can get a flu vaccine if :

  • have a serious long-term health condition
  • are having treatment or medication which means are at increased risk
  • are pregnant
  • are in long-term residential care
  • receive a carer's allowance, or are the main carer of an elderly or disabled person who may be at risk if get sick
  • live with or are in close contact with someone more likely to get infections (such as someone who has HIV, has had a transplant, is having certain treatments for cancer, lupus or rheumatoid arthritis)
  • are a frontline health or social care worker (if workplace doesn't offer an occupational health scheme)

A healthcare professional will ask some questions at the appointment to check if can have the vaccine.
