Book, change or cancel a Td/IPV vaccination appointment

The Td/IPV vaccine (also called the 3-in-1 teenage booster) helps protect against tetanus, diphtheria and polio.

Use this service to:

  • book, change or cancel an appointment
  • check your appointment details

Who can get the Td/IPV 3-in-1 teenage booster?

  • 16 to 24 year olds (up to the day before their 25th birthday)
  • Those who have not had a dose of the Td/IPV 3-in-1 teenage booster before

How it works

Most people can have the Td/IPV 3-in-1 teenage booster, including if they are pregnant or breastfeeding.

The service will check if they need a vaccine before booking.

We'll ask for your NHS number and/or personal details, including your date of birth, when booking.

To change or cancel an appointment, you'll need the booking reference number we sent you.

Booking with the NHS App

If you've signed up for the NHS App, you can use it to book a COVID-19 vaccination.

You can download or sign up for the NHS App if you have not used it before.

Book over the phone

You can call 119 free of charge to book over the phone if you cannot book online. You can ask for an interpreter if you need one.

If you have difficulties communicating or hearing

You can use textphone 18001 or the NHS 119 British Sign Language (BSL) interpreter service, or if you are a BSL user.
